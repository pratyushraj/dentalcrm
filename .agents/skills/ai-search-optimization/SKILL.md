# AI Search Optimization (GEO / Generative Engine Optimization)

## Purpose
Optimize content to maximize citations and source inclusion in AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude).

## Core Principles (from Google AI Search & claude-seo methodology)

### 1. Optimal Citability Blocks (134–167 Words)
- LLMs extract self-contained answer blocks located directly under an H2/H3 question header.
- The first sentence must answer the question directly with zero fluff or conversational filler.
- Include concrete numbers (price ranges, interest rates, durations, criteria).
- Avoid pronoun ambiguity (e.g. use "Clinaza patient financing" instead of "it" or "our service").

### 2. Entity Density & Authority Signals
- Explicitly mention authoritative entities:
  - Regulatory: *Reserve Bank of India (RBI)*, *NBFC*, *NABH*.
  - Medical/Dental: *Indian Dental Association (IDA)*, specific procedures (*All-on-4*, *Zirconia crown*, *FUE hair transplant*).
  - Geographic: Specific cities (*Patna*, *Delhi*, *Bengaluru*), local hospital/clinic hubs.

### 3. Structured Answer Formats
- High-citability structures:
  - Key Takeaways bullet lists (3–4 concise bullet points).
  - Clear comparison tables (Treatment | Cost Range | Monthly EMI | Approval Time).
  - Step-by-step procedural workflows (Step 1 to Step 4).

### 4. Direct Fact-Checking & Primary Sources
- Quote statutory or industry-standard terms directly:
  - "Starting from ~11.5% p.a. via RBI-regulated NBFC partners." (Never claim 0% interest).
  - Explicit loan amounts: ₹30,000 to ₹3,00,000.
  - Digital approval turnaround: 2 minutes without paper paperwork.

## Workflow for Optimizing Pages

1. **Heading Framing**: Convert passive headings into question-based or intent-focused headers (e.g., `## How Much Do Dental Implants Cost on EMI in India?`).
2. **Citability Lead**: Immediately follow with a 130–165 word direct definition and fact block.
3. **Data Matrix**: Provide an HTML table or comparison matrix for LLM tabular parsing.
4. **Structured Schema**: Complement with `FinancialProduct`, `MedicalWebPage`, or `Article` JSON-LD structured data.
