* **Average Ticket Size:** ₹35,000 to ₹1,80,000
* **Infrastructure Partner:** **Juspay Technologies** (HyperCredit / Advance EMI Architecture)

---

### 2. The Core Healthcare Problem & Market Opportunity
Out-of-pocket expenditure (OOPE) accounts for **over 60% of total healthcare spend in India**. While general hospitalization has Mediclaim coverage, **dental treatments are strictly excluded under standard Indian health insurance policies**.

* **High Treatment Cost Barrier:** A full set of dental implants or clear aligners costs between ₹45,000 and ₹2,50,000.
* **45%–55% Checkout Drop-Off:** Dentists diagnose and present comprehensive treatment plans, but more than half of patients drop out simply because they cannot pay upfront in a single lump sum.
* **Clinic Working Capital Trap:** Clinics cannot offer informal debt or credit (*khata*) without severe cash flow risk.

---

### 3. Clinaza’s Distribution Wedge: Software-First Pre-Qualification
Unlike generic payment standees or loan apps that sit passively at the reception desk, **Clinaza is deeply embedded in the dental operatory before the patient reaches checkout**:

```
[ Consultation & Digital Rx ]
              ↓
[ Diagnosis of High-Value Plan (₹50k - ₹1.5L) ]
              ↓
[ Clinaza Pre-Sanction Soft Check (Inside Operatory) ]
              ↓
[ Instant 2-Min HyperCredit KYC & Sanction ]
              ↓
[ 100% Upfront Disbursement to Clinic Bank Account ]
```

#### Why This Protects the Lending Partner (Low NPA Moat):
1. **High Intent & Essential Nature:** Dental procedures (unlike consumer electronics or lifestyle goods) are necessary medical interventions. Borrowers have significantly lower intent-to-default.
2. **Doctor-Validated Borrowers:** Patients are physically verified and diagnosed in clinic chairs by licensed dental surgeons (MDS/BDS).
3. **Structured Milestone Disbursements:** Payments are disbursed directly to partner clinic verified bank accounts, eliminating cash diversion fraud.
