Who IS Eligible (High Approval Rate)

✓
Age 21 to 65 Years: Salaried employees (private/govt) or Self-Employed / Business owners.
✓
Active Indian Bank Account: Savings or current account across all national & private banks (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, PNB, etc.).
✓
Digital e-NACH Ready: Active Netbanking or Debit Card to register paperless monthly auto-debit mandate.

Family Financing for Dependents: Earning spouse, parents, or family members can apply for students or homemakers.
❌

Who IS NOT Eligible (Will Be Rejected)

✕
Severe Loan Defaulters: Active NPAs, written-off credit accounts, or multiple 90+ day overdue defaults on bureau.
✕
Unlinked Mobile & Aadhaar: Patients unable to verify identity via Aadhaar OTP cannot complete digital e-KYC.
✕
Cash-Only Individuals: Patients without an active bank account, Netbanking, or Debit Card (cannot set up e-NACH).
✕
Minors Under 21: Individuals under 21 years cannot be the primary applicant under RBI lending rules.
✕
Foreign Nationals: Applicants without valid Indian PAN and Aadhaar credentials.
