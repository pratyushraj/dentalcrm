RISK & UNDERWRITING SAFEGUARDS
06 / 08

Robust Anti-Fraud Architecture: Preventing Merchant Cash-Out & Loan Round-Tripping

Direct clinic disbursement carries risks of collusion between doctors and fake patients. Clinaza builds 4 institutional defense layers to safeguard lending capital.

Pillar 01

Geofenced Dual-Presence

Financing can only trigger when both the patient’s mobile device and the clinic’s verified GPS terminal are within 50 meters of each other at checkout.

GPS + Device IP
Pillar 02

Clinical Digital Evidence

High-ticket cases (INR 50,000+) require mandatory digital OPG X-ray or intraoral scan submission before disbursement approval. Fake patients don't have broken teeth.

Digital OPG / Scan
Pillar 03

Milestone Tranche Disbursal

Disbursements above INR 1 Lakh are staged: 40% initial prep tranche, with remaining 60% released only upon verified lab crown/implant placement receipt.

Staged Tranches
Pillar 04

DCI Vetting & Reserve Fund

Doctors verified against Dental Council of India (DCI) register, clinic GST, and 6-mo bank statements, backed by a 5% rolling reserve hold on new clinic partners.

DCI State Registry
